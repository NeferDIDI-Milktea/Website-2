Our story

A little cup of happiness, brewed every day.

Neferdidi started with one simple idea: a great milk tea shouldn't be a special occasion. It should be the thing you grab on the way to work, the treat you share with friends, the little pause in a long day.

So we keep it simple — real tea brewed in small batches, fresh milk, real fruit, and pearls cooked fresh every single day. No shortcuts, no powders pretending to be tea.

  • Tea brewed fresh in-store, multiple times a day
  • Boba pearls cooked on-site, never re-heated
  • Real fruit purées — no artificial syrups
